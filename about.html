About Wodah Gabriel

A one-desk growth operation for creators — built so you can hand over the technical and promotional work and stay focused on what happens on camera.

Why this exists

Most creators are running two jobs at once: making the content, and running the business behind it — marketing, promotion, scheduling, reporting, cross-posting. Wodah Gabriel exists to take the second job off your plate, one platform at a time.

Instead of treating YouTube, Twitch, Kick, and TikTok as one generic "social media" bucket, each platform gets handled on its own terms — because what grows a YouTube channel doesn't grow a Twitch stream, and what works on TikTok doesn't automatically translate to Kick.

How it works

Every engagement starts with a channel audit: what's working, what's flat, and which service on the board would move the needle fastest. From there, it's a direct working relationship — one point of contact, clear reporting, and no work that happens without you knowing about it.

You can start with a single service, like promotion for one platform, or hand over the full board across all four platforms as your channel grows.
